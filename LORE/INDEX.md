# LORE index

Cross-project lessons — hazards, mechanisms and disciplines that are **not bound
to any one repo**. Per-project lessons live in that project's own `LORE/`.

One row per entry: the slug, which states the lesson as a claim, a one-line
gist, and its tags. Scan the section matching what you are about to touch.

Seeded 2026-08-11 by distilling the portable subset of `~/pivoker/LORE` (143
entries). Every entry names its pivoker origin in `sources:`.

**The gist's lead sentence is the whole message.** Anything that reads only the
lead — a person skimming, a context stamp that truncates — must not be
misdirected by it. Append later *incidents* to the tail; fold *corrections* into
the lead and leave the retired wording below it, marked `Superseded (<ref>):`.
